1. Real NASA Data Sources

The Earth Information Jukebox retrieves monthly climate observations directly from the NASA Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER) API and NASA EOSDIS GIBS (Global Imagery Browse Services):

  • T2M (°C): 2-Meter Surface Skin Temperature from NASA MERRA-2 assimilation model and AIRS sensor on the Aqua satellite.
  • PRECTOTCORR (mm/day): Corrected Precipitation Rate combining GPCP and satellite infrared/microwave precipitation estimates.
  • ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN (MJ/m²/day): All Sky Surface Shortwave Downward Irradiance derived from CERES, FLASHFlux, and MODIS satellites.
  • GISTEMP Climatological Baseline: 1981–2010 thirty-year baseline normals calculated per month to determine accurate thermal anomalies.

2. Live API Endpoint Format

https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/monthly/point?parameters=T2M,PRECTOTCORR,ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN&community=RE&longitude={lon}&latitude={lat}&start=1981&end=2025&format=JSON

When users click anywhere on the interactive map or enter coordinates, this REST endpoint fetches real 45-year monthly data points.

3. Sonification Architecture (Musical Harmony)

Rather than abrasive noise, climate data is harmonized into an evocative D Major Pentatonic / Lydian scale (D, E, F#, G#, A, B, C#) with gentle stereo reverb and delay:

  • Surface Temperature: Mapped to an Analog Lead Synth whose pitch ascends and lowpass filter frequency opens (400 Hz to 4,200 Hz) as temperatures increase.
  • Precipitation: Mapped to Acoustic Marimba arpeggios where rainfall intensity controls note density and ping-pong stereo shimmer.
  • Solar Insolation: Drives the harmonic chord richness and envelope swell of a lush cosmic synthesizer pad.
  • Climate Anomaly Alert: Triggers a crystal bell chime whenever regional warming surpasses +1.5°C above baseline or historic deluges occur.
